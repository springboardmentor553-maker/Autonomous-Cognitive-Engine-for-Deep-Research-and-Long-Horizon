**Findings:**

1. **Personalization and Adaptation**: AI agents are increasingly being used to provide personalized learning experiences, adapting to individual students' needs, abilities, and learning styles (e.g., [1], [2]).
2. **Intelligent Tutoring Systems**: AI-powered tutoring systems have been shown to be effective in improving student outcomes, particularly in math and science subjects (e.g., [3], [4]).
3. **Natural Language Processing**: AI agents are being used to support language learning, with applications such as chatbots and virtual assistants (e.g., [5], [6]).
4. **Teacher Support**: AI agents are being explored as tools to support teachers, including grading, feedback, and classroom management (e.g., [7], [8]).
5. **Ethical Considerations**: Studies have highlighted the need for careful consideration of ethical issues, such as bias, privacy, and transparency, when integrating AI agents in educational settings (e.g., [9], [10]).
6. **Student Engagement**: AI agents have been found to increase student engagement and motivation, particularly when used in conjunction with gamification and interactive learning activities (e.g., [11], [12]).

**Key Trends:**

1. **Increased Focus on Human-AI Collaboration**: Studies are shifting from solely focusing on AI agents as replacements for human teachers to exploring how AI can augment and support human teaching.
2. **Growing Emphasis on Equity and Inclusion**: Researchers are highlighting the need to ensure that AI agents are designed and implemented in ways that promote equity, inclusion, and accessibility.
3. **Expansion of AI Applications**: AI agents are being applied to a wider range of educational contexts, including special education, language learning, and professional development.

**Next Steps:**

1. **Investigate the Long-Term Impact**: Conduct longitudinal studies to examine the sustained effects of AI agent integration on student outcomes and teacher practices.
2. **Develop Frameworks for Ethical AI Integration**: Create guidelines and frameworks to support educators and developers in designing and implementing AI agents in ways that prioritize ethics, equity, and transparency.
3. **Explore AI Agent-Human Teacher Collaboration**: Investigate the potential benefits and challenges of collaborative teaching models that combine the strengths of human teachers and AI agents.

**Limitations:**

1. **Limited Generalizability**: The review is based on a specific set of studies and may not be generalizable to all educational contexts or settings.
2. **Rapidly Evolving Field**: The field of AI in education is rapidly evolving, and new developments may have occurred since the completion of this review.

**Conclusion:**

The integration of AI agents in educational settings is a rapidly growing area of research, with key trends and findings highlighting the potential benefits and challenges of AI-powered education.
