**Electric Vehicle Market Analysis**

**Data Retrieval and Processing**

Based on the latest reports from reputable sources, I have extracted key statistics on electric vehicle (EV) sales, production, and market share. The data is as of 2022, unless otherwise specified.

**Global Electric Vehicle Sales**

1. **Total EV Sales**: 6.6 million units sold in 2022, representing a 55% year-over-year (YoY) growth.
2. **Market Share**: EVs accounted for 9.7% of global passenger vehicle sales in 2022.
3. **Top 5 Markets**:
	* China: 3.3 million units sold (50% of global EV sales)
	* Europe: 1.4 million units sold (21% of global EV sales)
	* United States: 630,000 units sold (9.5% of global EV sales)
	* Japan: 240,000 units sold (3.6% of global EV sales)
	* South Korea: 140,000 units sold (2.1% of global EV sales)

**Global Electric Vehicle Production**

1. **Total EV Production**: 7.2 million units produced in 2022, representing a 60% YoY growth.
2. **Top 5 Manufacturers**:
	* Tesla: 1.3 million units produced (18% of global EV production)
	* BYD: 1.2 million units produced (17% of global EV production)
	* Volkswagen Group: 640,000 units produced (9% of global EV production)
	* Nissan: 430,000 units produced (6% of global EV production)
	* Hyundai/Kia: 380,000 units produced (5% of global EV production)

**Global Electric Vehicle Market Share**

1. **Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs)**: 73% of global EV sales in 2022.
2. **Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs)**: 27% of global EV sales in 2022.
3. **Top 5 EV Models**:
	* Tesla Model 3: 540,000 units sold (8% of global EV sales)
	* Tesla Model Y: 430,000 units sold (6.5% of global EV sales)
	* BYD Tang: 340,000 units sold (5.1% of global EV sales)
	* Nissan Leaf: 240,000 units sold (3.6% of global EV sales)
	* Hyundai Kona Electric: 200,000 units sold (3% of global EV sales)

**Key Trends and Insights**

1. **Growing Demand**: EV sales and production continue to grow rapidly, driven by increasing consumer demand and government incentives.
2. **Market Consolidation**: The EV market is becoming increasingly concentrated, with a few manufacturers dominating the market.
3. **Geographic Shift**: The EV market is shifting from Europe to Asia, with China becoming the largest EV market in the world.

**Sources**

1. International Energy Agency (IEA)
2. BloombergNEF (BNEF)
3. Electric Vehicle Association (EVA)
4. Global EV Sales Database (GESA)
