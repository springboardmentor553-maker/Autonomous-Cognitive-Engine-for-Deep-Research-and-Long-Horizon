**Research Note: Evaluating the Impact of AI Agents on Student Learning Outcomes and Educational Equity**

**Assumptions:**

1. AI agents are increasingly being integrated into educational settings to support student learning.
2. The impact of AI agents on student learning outcomes and educational equity is not yet fully understood.
3. A framework for evaluating the impact of AI agents on student learning outcomes and educational equity is necessary to inform effective implementation and policy decisions.

**Findings:**

1. **Learning Outcomes:** AI agents can positively impact student learning outcomes by providing personalized support, real-time feedback, and adaptive assessments. However, the effectiveness of AI agents in improving learning outcomes depends on factors such as the quality of the AI system, teacher support, and student engagement.
2. **Educational Equity:** AI agents can exacerbate existing educational inequities if they are not designed and implemented with equity considerations in mind. For example, AI agents may perpetuate biases in data and algorithms, leading to unequal access to educational opportunities and resources.
3. **Key Factors:** The impact of AI agents on student learning outcomes and educational equity is influenced by key factors, including:
	* **AI system design:** The design of the AI system, including its algorithms, data, and user interface, can affect its impact on student learning outcomes and educational equity.
	* **Teacher support:** Teacher support and training are essential for effective integration of AI agents into educational settings.
	* **Student engagement:** Student engagement and motivation are critical for maximizing the benefits of AI agents.
	* **Contextual factors:** Contextual factors, such as school resources, student demographics, and community support, can influence the impact of AI agents on student learning outcomes and educational equity.
4. **Evaluation Methods:** A range of evaluation methods can be used to assess the impact of AI agents on student learning outcomes and educational equity, including:
	* **Quantitative methods:** Statistical analysis of student learning outcomes, such as test scores and grades.
	* **Qualitative methods:** Case studies, interviews, and observations to gather insights into the experiences and perspectives of students, teachers, and other stakeholders.
	* **Mixed-methods approaches:** Combining quantitative and qualitative methods to provide a comprehensive understanding of the impact of AI agents.

**Framework for Evaluating the Impact of AI Agents:**

1. **Define Evaluation Questions:** Identify specific evaluation questions related to the impact of AI agents on student learning outcomes and educational equity.
2. **Assess AI System Design:** Evaluate the design of the AI system, including its algorithms, data, and user interface, to identify potential biases and areas for improvement.
3. **Collect and Analyze Data:** Collect and analyze data on student learning outcomes, teacher support, student engagement, and contextual factors to assess the impact of AI agents.
4. **Use Mixed-Methods Approaches:** Use a combination of quantitative and qualitative methods to provide a comprehensive understanding of the impact of AI agents.
5. **Consider Contextual Factors:** Consider the contextual factors that may influence the impact of AI agents, such as school resources, student demographics, and community support.
6. **Monitor and Adjust:** Continuously monitor the impact of AI agents and adjust the evaluation framework as needed to ensure that it remains relevant and effective.

**Next Steps:**

1. **Conduct Further Research:** Conduct further research to refine the framework and gather more evidence on the impact of AI agents on student learning outcomes and educational equity.
2. **Develop Evaluation Tools:** Develop evaluation tools and protocols to support the implementation of the framework.
3. **Pilot Test the Framework:** Pilot test the framework in a range of educational settings to refine its effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.
4. **Disseminate Findings:** Disseminate the findings and recommendations to educators, policymakers, and other stakeholders to inform effective implementation and policy decisions.
